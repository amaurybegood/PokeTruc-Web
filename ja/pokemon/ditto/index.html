This card was given out as part of the Ditto QR Stamp Rally during the Pokémon Town 2025 event held as part of the METAMONG PROJECT between April 25 and May 18, 2025, as long as stocks lasted. Attendees would receive this card for visiting at least one of the six locations announced for the rally, and would also receive Pokémon Town 2025 stickers depicting a banner similar to the illustration on this card for visiting all six locations.
