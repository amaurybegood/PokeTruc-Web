#025

Pikachu

PokéTruc has catalogued 153 exclusive Pikachu TCG card illustrations, spanning 1996 to 2026. The collection includes 106 Japanese-exclusive cards, 14 Asian-exclusive cards, 10 English-exclusive cards, 10 Chinese-exclusive cards, 4 Indonesian-exclusive cards, 2 Spanish-exclusive cards, 1 Korean-exclusive card, 1 German-exclusive card, 1 French-exclusive card, 1 Italian-exclusive card, 1 Portuguese-exclusive card, 1 Polish-exclusive card and 1 Western-exclusive card. Illustrated by 65 different artists including Kouki Saitou, Sanosuke Sakuma and Naoki Saito.
