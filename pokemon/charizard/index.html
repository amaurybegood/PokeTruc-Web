Charizard

PokéTruc has catalogued 13 exclusive Charizard TCG card illustrations, spanning 1996 to 2025. The collection includes 7 Japanese-exclusive cards, 3 Western-exclusive cards, 1 English-exclusive card, 1 Chinese-exclusive card and 1 Asian-exclusive card. Illustrated by 11 different artists including Ken Sugimori, aky CG Works and Aya Kusube.
