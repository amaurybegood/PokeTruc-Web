Fearow

PokéTruc has catalogued 4 exclusive Fearow TCG card illustrations, spanning 1996 to 2006. The collection includes 4 Japanese-exclusive cards. Illustrated by 3 different artists including Atsuko Nishida, Sumiyoshi Kizuki and Shin-ichi Yoshida.
