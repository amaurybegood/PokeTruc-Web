#151

Mew

PokéTruc has catalogued 11 exclusive Mew TCG card illustrations, spanning 1996 to 2024. The collection includes 9 Japanese-exclusive cards, 1 Chinese-exclusive card and 1 German-exclusive card. Illustrated by 11 different artists including Ken Sugimori, Hironobu Yoshida and Hajime Kusajima.
