Eevee

PokéTruc has catalogued 23 exclusive Eevee TCG card illustrations, spanning 1996 to 2025. The collection includes 20 Japanese-exclusive cards, 2 Chinese-exclusive cards and 1 Western-exclusive card. Illustrated by 16 different artists including Kouki Saitou, Toshinao Aoki and Mitsuhiro Arita.
