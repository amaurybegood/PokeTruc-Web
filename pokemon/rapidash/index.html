Rapidash

PokéTruc has catalogued 3 exclusive Rapidash TCG card illustrations, spanning 1998 to 2020. The collection includes 2 Japanese-exclusive cards and 1 English-exclusive card. Illustrated by 3 different artists including Atsuko Nishida, Hajime Kusajima and aoki.
