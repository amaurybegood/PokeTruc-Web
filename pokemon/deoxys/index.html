PokéTruc has catalogued 9 exclusive Deoxys TCG card illustrations, spanning 1996 to 2019. The collection includes 6 Japanese-exclusive cards, 2 Western-exclusive cards and 1 Asian-exclusive card. Illustrated by 7 different artists including Nakaoka, Mitsuhiro Arita and K. Hoshiba.
