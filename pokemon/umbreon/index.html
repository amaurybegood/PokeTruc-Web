Umbreon

PokéTruc has catalogued 6 exclusive Umbreon TCG card illustrations, spanning 2001 to 2025. The collection includes 3 Japanese-exclusive cards, 1 Chinese-exclusive card, 1 Western-exclusive card and 1 Asian-exclusive card. Illustrated by 5 different artists including Mitsuhiro Arita, Hajime Kusajima and 5ban Graphics.
