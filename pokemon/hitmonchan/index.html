Hitmonchan

PokéTruc has catalogued 3 exclusive Hitmonchan TCG card illustrations, spanning 1998 to 2011. The collection includes 2 Japanese-exclusive cards and 1 Western-exclusive card. Illustrated by 3 different artists including Nobuyuki Habu, Yuka Morii and Kouki Saitou.
